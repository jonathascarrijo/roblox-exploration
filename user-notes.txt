I'd like to explore a design direction.
Snake Show coulbe become a fantasy creature collection game.
So in every minigame either the loyals or the snakes collect the creature.
In Prize Lift (now Moonmop Lift), if the loyals won, only those who deliver the Moonmop will be granted one.
Same with Snakes. If the snakes won, only those who dropped a Moonmop while armed will be given one.
Each creature will come in a variety of colors, some colors rarer than others.
At the lobby, there will be an area dedicated for creature exchanging.
Each challenge will award its own kind of fantasy creature.
We'll need an interface for the player to see their collected creatures. They can see each baby creature individually and pet them.
For each challenge, we'd have to think how to insert the magic creature into the plot.
