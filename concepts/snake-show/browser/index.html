YOUR FIRST EPISODE

Keep your friends close.
Watch their cables.

  1. Know your role. Six Loyals protect the treasures. Two Snakes secretly sabotage. Snakes need two heists and one survivor after the last vote.
  2. Work your cable. Hold Space or Pull to raise your end; release to lower it. Tilt to guide the ball inward, then slow it near center. Every corner must reach the gold delivery line.
  3. Save the capsule. If it slips, press C or Catch when the needle is in the gold zone. You get one tap per fall. Someone else can still save after a miss.
  4. Play the Snake. Hold R or Rig for 1.5 seconds to arm the team’s one attempt. Your motor doubles for 5 seconds. A rigged, uncaught spill is a heist; lava contact is only a failed lift.
  5. Spot when the cast is odd. Every lift has exactly two motors. If your partner is voted out and the cast is odd, you spot the next act: no console, but you can walk into any rescue area to Catch there. Your longest stay and your taps become a receipt.
  6. Follow the evidence. Finish early to see the pictures while the others play. When every lift finishes or 45 seconds run out, you have 35 seconds to look at the pictures and vote. Everyone sees the votes as they arrive. Tap a face to vote; change it until you lock it or time is up. When everyone locks, voting ends early. Every vote starts as a hunch. Tap “I’m sure!” to show certainty. You cannot vote for yourself. A tie gets a runoff. Voted out? Follow the episode backstage or start again.

Walk with WASD / arrows or tap the courtyard. E uses a nearby console. Escape pauses. Sound is optional. Practice uses the same physics with one Loyal bot.

This is an offline browser adaptation for one player. Bots and balance are experimental; there is no online matchmaking or persistent economy.
