YOUR FIRST ROUND

Keep your friends close.
Watch their cables.

  1. Know your team. Six Keepers protect the fair nursery. Two Tricksters secretly divert pods to the Mischief Court. Tricksters need two diversions and one survivor after the last vote. Both nurseries care for the babies.
  2. Lift Moonmop safely. Hold Space or Pull to raise your cable; release to lower it. Keep the protected pod near center. Both tray corners must reach the moon-nest line.
  3. Catch a slipping pod. Press C or Catch in the gold zone. One tap per fault; others can still save after a miss. An unrescued pod disappears into the opaque safety housing above the theatrical lava. Neither route hurts Moonmop.
  5. Spot when the cast is odd. If your partner was voted out, you may spot the next trial. Walk into rescue areas to Catch. Every lift still has exactly two operators. Spotters still vote and can earn their team’s reward.
  6. Read clues and vote together. Voting opens immediately when the lifts finish, with one shared 35-second clock. Everyone sees choices, pointing and counts live. Tap a face to place or change your hunch; “I’m sure!” shows confidence without extra vote weight. You can skip, change your mind, then lock when ready. If everyone locks, the vote ends early. No self-votes. Ties get a 10-second runoff; a second tie removes nobody.
  7. Win with your team. Adopt one baby. Every participating winner receives one Moonmop, including voted-out teammates. Personal deliveries, saves and Rig credit are not required. Practice and watching award none. Your owned babies are never put at risk.

WASD / arrows or tap to walk. E uses a console. Escape pauses. A removed player can follow public clues, or return to the fair while the bots finish the round in this tab.

One player and local bots. Palette odds, participation rules, badges and social interactions are prototype choices. Nursery progress saves only in this browser; there is no online play or real player exchange.
