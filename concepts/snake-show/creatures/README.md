## Maintaining the records

Use the [creature template](_template.md). Link to the associated challenge and add both catalogue entries. Record unknown anatomy, colors, or behavior as open; do not fill them with invented decisions just to complete a page. An image filename or earlier prop does not establish a selected species design.

One species–color combination is a variant. Two owned babies with the same species and color remain distinct individuals. Species pages describe appearance and behavior; they do not redefine the shared reward quantity or who wins an adoption.
