# Chimechick

10 September 2026 · Creature proposal; not selected, modeled, or validated

**Associated challenge:** [Stamp & Spark](../challenges/stamp-and-spark.md). **Game:** [The Midnight Fair](../midnight-fair-design-v3.md). This record owns the creature's identity and presentation; challenge controls and physical rules live in the linked challenge document.

## Identity and challenge story

**Chimechick**, an enchanted ceramic bird, awakens after its shell and chime are assembled. The press works before the baby is alive.

The description above is the current extent of the proposed identity. Detailed anatomy, movement, and a species-specific character sheet remain to design. Earlier challenge illustrations depict reference cargo and are not approved character art for this species.

## Nursery behavior

Sings two notes and listens for an echo.

All colors use the same quality of interaction. The shared nursery and companion behavior is defined in the [main brief](../midnight-fair-design-v3.md#10-my-nursery-and-showing-off); species-specific behavior belongs here. No care timer, compulsory growth, or gameplay advantage is introduced by this record.
