# Reference art

These are reference images and dated generation notes, not a completed game asset library. Read the associated [challenge](../challenges/README.md) or [creature](../creatures/README.md) record to determine what is selected, proposed, or superseded.

- **`creatures/`** contains the Moonmop character studies, original mascot alternatives, and generation notes. The [long-ear Moonmop study](creatures/moonmop-design-pass-v3-2026-09-10.md) is the selected character direction. Earlier versions and Glowtail/Pebblewing remain historical exploration.
- **`challenges/`** contains the earlier challenge illustrations and their [generation notes](challenges/generation-notes-2026-09-09.md). Many depict treasure or prior mascots in the villa. They illustrate mechanical staging; they are not adopted character art for the newer species proposals.
- The former root-level [Moonmop Lift villa artwork](../archive/artwork/moonmop-lift-villa-2026-09-10.png) is in the archive because its overall scene predates The Midnight Fair.

Use descriptive names and retain a date/version for revisions. Put generation notes beside the relevant images, and link new selections from their creature or challenge record. Preserve prior art and its provenance when the selected direction changes.
