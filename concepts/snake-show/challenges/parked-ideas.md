# Variants and parked challenge ideas

10 September 2026 · No new family or creature selected

These entries preserve the current disposition of ideas that are not standalone candidates in the [main catalogue](README.md). Their complete earlier descriptions remain in the [family exploration](../explorations/pair-challenge-families.md). This file does not resolve their blockers or promote them into the release roster.

## Rail Cart

Two thrusters steer one cart along a rail using the sum-and-difference relationship of Moonmop Lift turned on its side. This remains a presentation variant of the lift model, not a mechanically distinct challenge family. It would use the underlying [Moonmop Lift](moonmop-lift.md) species unless a later design explicitly changes that status.

Adding a new creature solely for a presentation change would redefine what counts as a challenge-specific species. Leave that decision open. [Original entry](../explorations/pair-challenge-families.md#rail-cart).

## Vault Whisper

One player sees a code and the other operates a dial. The signal itself must work as a game action without assuming chat. A wrong signal can cause unmetered sabotage that an attentive partner cannot prevent, violating the shared counterplay requirement. Making the answer verifiable can instead make the signaler unnecessary; adding naturally confusing decoys risks making honest play frustrating.

This remains parked until a finite signaling control, readable ordinary counterplay, and acceptable player experience are specified. Platform suitability of any communication interface needs separate verification before implementation. No creature is assigned. [Original entry](../explorations/pair-challenge-families.md#vault-whisper-signal-and-act).

## Double Stamp

Both players press within a short window to seal a cargo crate. A missed or late press currently creates no fault for Catch and permits disruption without a bounded Rig attempt. It remains parked as a standalone family.

It could be explored as a brief step inside another challenge, such as sealing a nest at the end of a run, but that does not create a separate reward or selected creature. [Original entry](../explorations/pair-challenge-families.md#double-stamp-simultaneity).
