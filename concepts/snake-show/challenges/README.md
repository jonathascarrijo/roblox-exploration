## Variants and parked ideas

[Rail Cart, Vault Whisper, and Double Stamp](parked-ideas.md) have no additional selected creature or place in the release roster. Rail Cart reuses the lift relationship; the other two retain their unresolved mechanical blockers.

## Choosing the next comparison

Validate Moonmop Lift with real pairs first. Then compare candidates on honest cooperation, both jobs' prevention opportunities, readable clues, instruction time, and voluntary repeat play. The earlier study favored gate-and-runner exploration, with Vault Volley needing a counterplay repair and Bubble Trouble as another distinct relationship. The collection pass also highlighted Bubble Trouble and Critter Crossing for their different creature behaviors. V3 does not choose an implementation order between those leads.

The [family exploration](../explorations/pair-challenge-families.md) retains the comparative research, candidate ranking, and unresolved playtest questions. Continue comparisons there or in a new exploration; edit the individual challenge records for changes to their current rules. Add a new page using the [challenge template](_template.md), and update this index plus the linked creature record.
