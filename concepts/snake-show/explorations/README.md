# Design explorations and supporting analysis

These documents still inform The Midnight Fair. They hold open alternatives, comparisons, research interpretation, and the reasoning behind decisions. Inclusion here does not mean every proposal is current or adopted. The [design brief](../midnight-fair-design-v3.md) governs current direction and decision status; the [challenge](../challenges/README.md) and [creature](../creatures/README.md) records own their respective details.

## Where to continue the work

Use explorations to compare alternatives and record why a decision matters. Update the relevant current design document when a decision is made, and link that decision from the exploration. Keep dated analysis intact; add a dated follow-up or a new pass when developing it further.

A mixed-status document can remain useful after some of its rules are replaced. Mark those sections and link to the replacement instead of archiving the whole document. Move an exploration to the [archive](../archive/README.md) only when its useful content has been retired or fully replaced. Consolidation into a shorter brief does not, by itself, replace the reasoning or settle open questions.
